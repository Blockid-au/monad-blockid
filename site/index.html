Know the business you own.

If you own 2% of a private company, today you own a PDF. Business Passport gives every shareholder a live share register on Monad, monthly updates drafted by AI from the company's own data and signed off by a director, and dividends paid to every wallet in a single transaction.

The problem

Private companies are the biggest asset class nobody can see into.

Angel, employee and family shareholders hold a certificate and wait. Australia alone has millions of private companies, and their registers live in spreadsheets reconciled by hand.

01

No live register

Ownership is a PDF certificate and a spreadsheet. Nobody can check it.

02

Updates you can't trust

Updates arrive when the founder remembers. Investors can't tell data from marketing.

03

AI makes it worse

AI can write a convincing update in seconds. Without evidence and a responsible signer, it is just more noise.

04

Slow dividends

Paid by bank transfer, months late, holder by holder.

How it works on Monad

AI proposes. A director signs. Monad records. Every holder gets paid.

The AI never holds a key and never moves money. Its output becomes a shareholder record only after a named human approves it, and the dividend contract refuses to pay until that approval is on-chain.

MonadAnchors and paysUpdateAnchored event; BatchDividend pays every holder in one transaction.

ShareRegister

Permissioned ERC-20 per share class (BlockIDShareToken + IdentityRegistry). Only verified wallets can hold shares. Mirrors the legal register: proof of ownership, not a token sale.

UpdateAnchor New

AI-drafted update recorded with an evidence-confidence score and evidenced / missing counts; anchored only when a director of that company approves. Signatures from EOAs or ERC-1271 smart accounts; nonces and deadlines stop replay.

BatchDividend New

One director-declared transaction pays a stablecoin dividend pro-rata to every holder. The contract checks the holder list is complete and duplicate-free against total supply. Per-holder events are the payment record.

    Paying every holder in one transaction costs cents on Monad.

    Cost that stays flat

    Under a cent for 20 holders, a few cents for 200. Small companies can afford monthly payouts and weekly re-anchoring.

    Room in the block

    About 350 holders fit in one transaction (30M gas per transaction), and 150M-gas blocks hold several companies' payouts at once. Larger registers split into batches or use the Merkle distributor.

    Fast finality

    Sub-second blocks: the director sees the payout confirmed while still on the approval screen, and shareholders see it moments later.

    Why Track 4

    A trust layer for what companies say about themselves.

    Human-gated AI attestations

    AI output becomes a record only after a named director signs. The recorder can never approve its own draft (four-eyes, enforced on-chain).

    Smart-account signers

    Directors approve with an EIP-712 signature checked through ERC-1271, so passkey (P256 / WebAuthn) smart accounts work without a seed phrase. Anyone can relay it.

    Identity of the legal object

    Verified-wallet register mirroring a real company's share register, with the constitution and valuation report hashes on-chain.
