The product

Three contracts on Monad.

ShareRegister

Permissioned ERC-20 per share class. Only verified wallets hold shares. Mirrors the company's legal register: proof of ownership, not a token sale.

UpdateAnchor new

AI-drafted update with an evidence-confidence score. Anchored only when a director of that company approves. EOA or passkey smart-account signatures.

BatchDividend new

One transaction pays a stablecoin dividend pro-rata to every holder. The contract checks nobody is left out and nobody is counted twice.

Why Track 4

A trust layer for what companies
say about themselves.

Human-gated AI attestations

AI output becomes a record only after a named director signs. Four-eyes enforced on-chain: the recorder can't approve its own draft.

Passkey-ready signers

EIP-712 approvals checked through ERC-1271: P256 / WebAuthn smart accounts work with no seed phrase. Anyone can relay; nonces and deadlines stop replay.

Identity of a legal object

A verified-wallet register mirroring a real company's register, with constitution and valuation report hashes on-chain.
