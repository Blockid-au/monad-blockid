BlockID Business Passport on Monad
Know the business you own: on-chain share register, director-approved AI updates, and dividends to every wallet in one transaction.
Track 4 — Trust, Identity & AI Infrastructure
Private companies are the biggest asset class nobody can see into. Their angel, employee and family shareholders hold a PDF certificate, get an update when the founder remembers, and wait months for dividends by bank transfer. AI makes this worse, not better: a convincing update now takes seconds to write, with no evidence and no one accountable for it. Business Passport turns a company's register and reporting into a trust primitive on Monad: • ShareRegister — a permissioned ERC-20 per share class (BlockIDShareToken + IdentityRegistry). Only verified wallets can hold shares; it mirrors the company's legal register as proof of ownership, not a token sale. • UpdateAnchor (new) — BlockID's analysis pipeline drafts the monthly update from the company's own data and marks every claim evidenced or missing, with an evidence-confidence score. The issuer service records the draft; it is anchored only when a director of that company approves it. A director approves directly or with an EIP-712 signature; smart-account signatures are checked through ERC-1271, so passkey (P256 / WebAuthn) accounts work without a seed phrase. The recorder can never approve its own draft. • BatchDividend (new) — one director-declared transaction pays a stablecoin dividend pro-rata to every holder. The contract checks on-chain that the holder list is complete and duplicate-free against total supply. In this build the demo script pays only after the update declaring the dividend is director-approved; moving that check into the contract is the next change. Why Monad: on Monad testnet our deployed BatchDividend paid 20 holders in one transaction for 1.49M gas — 0.157 MON, about US$0.004. Extrapolated, 200 holders cost about US$0.04 in a single transaction, versus 200 separate claim transactions with a Merkle distributor, or about US$160 on Ethereum L1 at 10 gwei. About 400 holders fit in one transaction, and sub-second blocks let the director see the payout confirmed on the approval screen. Anyone can check an update without trusting us: monad.blockid.au hashes the update in the browser and calls UpdateAnchor.verify on Monad directly. Live on Monad testnet (chain 10143): share register, UpdateAnchor and BatchDividend deployed; the AI-drafted update was approved by a separate director wallet and the dividend paid to 20 holders in one transaction. Demo company and generated sample holder wallets. No real users yet. Testnet demo; not an offer of securities or financial advice.
New on branch `monad` (github.com/Blockid-au/monad-blockid/tree/monad), from 8 Oct 2026: - contracts/src/UpdateAnchor.sol — AI-drafted update recorded with evidence confidence; director approval directly or by EIP-712 signature (EOA or ERC-1271 smart account, nonces + deadlines); four-eyes enforced on-chain; verify(id, hash) view. - contracts/src/BatchDividend.sol — pro-rata stablecoin dividend to every holder in one transaction; on-chain completeness (sum of balances = total supply) and uniqueness (strictly ascending holders) checks; dust returned. - contracts/test/Monad.t.sol — 15 new tests (52 Foundry tests in total) incl. smart-account signature, replay and expiry; gas benchmark for 10 / 50 / 200 holders. - contracts/script/MonadPassport.s.sol + scripts/monad-demo.sh — deploy, record the update, director approves from a separate wallet, dividend paid only if approved, results published for the site. - Deployed to Monad testnet and ran the full flow: propose → director approve → one-transaction dividend to 20 holders (1.49M gas). - monad.blockid.au — pitch site with in-browser verification against Monad RPC, live contract and payout data, pitch deck.
